# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- Add your own rules on top of the plugin: `cos overlay init` creates one starter file per role, and updates never touch them.
- Your rules win where they differ from the package's, so you take every new release without merging anything.
- A team can share one set of rules by pointing everyone at the same folder or repository.
- A new guide, "Add your own rules", explains what belongs in your overlay and what to send upstream.
