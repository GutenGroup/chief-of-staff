# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- The Workspace panel now comes standard in the desktop app, no separate opt-in step.
- The Chief of Staff installs the panel on its own when it finds it missing and tells you once to restart the app.
- Releases now arrive every 7 to 10 days with the changes collected since the last one, instead of one per change.
