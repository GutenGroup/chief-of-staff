# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- `cos overlay init` always creates your private rules folder, `~/.claude/cos-overlay/`; it never points you at a shared one.
- Sharing rules with a team is now a clearly optional step in the guide, with what to share and what to keep private.
- Your rules still win where they differ from the package's, and updates never touch them.
