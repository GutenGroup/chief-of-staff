# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- Every role (Chief of Staff, Production VP, each COO) learns from your corrections: a dated lesson, a check that runs every pass, a weekly scorecard and a monthly knowledge test.
- A full Production VP that keeps quality high across all your products and coaches their COOs.
- An operating guide: where each role's memory lives, when it clears, which model and effort each job gets.
- Optional Jev decisions (route, effort, safe, retry) for fewer needless questions and retries; everything works without it.
- Every change is checked automatically and published as a new release when green.
