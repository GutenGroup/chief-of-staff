# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- New standard role: the Communication VP handles all your communication, next to the Production VP.
- Tell it who gets what and where: Google Chat spaces, botreeve groups or a shared folder, behind one connector interface.
- It learns what it may send: say "send without approval" or "always verify" once per person, group or kind of message.
- Plug in your own drafting tool, or let it draft; every message is signed as written by your Communication VP.
- botreeve is the bridge: each person keeps their own Chief of Staff, and teams connect through shared groups.
