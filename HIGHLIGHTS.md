# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- A standard sidebar layout: the Chief of Staff, every VP and every COO are pinned together in one group named "⭐ Chief of Staff".
- The Chief of Staff session now has a bold title with gold stars, easy to spot at the top of your sidebar.
- Role titles are shorter: "Production VP", "Communication VP", "Sales COO" instead of "CoS · Sales COO".
- Updating sets this up for you: the first pass after the update renames, pins and groups every role once.
- New `cos layout` command prints the layout the Chief of Staff expects, so you can compare it with your sidebar.
