# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- Keep your own rules in a local overlay folder: every skill reads it after its own text, and updates never touch it.
- The Chief of Staff's orders carry your authority, so COOs act at once; only signing and paying wait for your click.
- No more being handed keys, logins or setup steps the system can find and do itself.
- Safer restarts: after a clear the Chief of Staff recognizes itself, and the app's session-start rules are handled for you.
- Faster delivery: ready pull requests, same-pass re-dispatch of failed jobs, and COOs that own their project's knowledge.
- Installs report the right version: the plugin version now always matches the release tag.
