# Highlights

The "## Highlights" section of the next GitHub Release: 3-6 plain-language bullets of what users can now do.
Update it in the same change as the feature. CI fails when there are fewer than 3 or more than 6 bullets.

- The system now decides and does the work itself: only legal and money decisions reach you, each as one ready-to-click packet with a recommendation, the values to check and one link to the final step.
- Built to run unattended in bypass permissions mode; `cos status` and the Workspace panel tell you when a session still runs with prompts.
- Hooks stop untagged questions to you and turns that end on "want me to...?", and when you say "just do it" the system does it and learns the lesson.
- New `cos precheck` looks up your decisions, lessons and knowledge sources before any ask, send or access change, so the system stops acting on stale information.
- The Communication VP now sends status, scheduling and replies to your known contacts on its own; only messages that commit money or legal terms come to you.
- The Workspace panel shows what the system decided for you, each with an Undo button.
